# Project Proposal

## Approved Project Title: 
«Разработка платформы для отслеживания жизненного цикла продукции» (Development of a Platform for Product Lifecycle Tracking)

## Team members: Ingkar Adilbek and Farida Dovletbayeva

### Supervisor: Shyngys Alshynov

## Problem Statement: 
Information about a product's lifecycle, including its registration, movement, maintenance, transfers, and retirement, may be stored in separate records or information systems maintained by different organizations. As a result, retrieving the available history of a particular product may require manually locating and comparing information from multiple sources. The project will investigate how lifecycle information is currently recorded and managed, as well as which product categories and organizational processes could benefit from a unified tracking platform.

## Target users: 
Employees of organizations responsible for registering products, managing their movement and condition, recording lifecycle operations, and retrieving historical information. Specific user roles may include inventory managers, service personnel, and other employees involved in product lifecycle management. System administrators will manage accounts and access permissions.

## Aim: 
To design and develop a web-based prototype for recording, managing, and retrieving product lifecycle information through a unified system.

## Objectives:

1.	investigate existing lifecycle tracking approaches, potential users, and relevant application scenarios.
2.	define functional and non-functional requirements based on the selected project scope.
3.	design the platform architecture, data model, lifecycle events, and access-control mechanisms.
4.	implement core functionality for product registration, event recording, history viewing, search, and basic reporting.
5.	test the platform's functionality, data consistency, and access restrictions.
6.	evaluate the prototype using predefined tasks and a suitable comparison baseline.
Expected Outcomes: a functioning web-based prototype demonstrating product registration, lifecycle event tracking, and history retrieval, accompanied by requirements, architectural documentation, test data, and verification results.

## Project scope: 
The proposed platform will focus on maintaining structured records of products and their lifecycle events. The preliminary functionality includes product identification and registration, event history, current status, search, user roles, access control, and basic reporting. The system will be designed with the possibility of supporting different product categories. However, the specific categories, lifecycle stages, and organizational workflows supported by the first prototype will be determined following further research. Integration with external business systems, automated data collection, and advanced industry-specific functionality are outside the preliminary scope.
